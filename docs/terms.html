Volvo to Tibber Sync Terms of Use

Effective 10 November 2026

Use of the integration

Volvo to Tibber Sync is a self-hosted project for home charging. Use it only with a vehicle and accounts you own or are authorized to manage. Your use of the Volvo and Tibber services is subject to their respective terms.

How the integration uses data

The integration uses the vehicle identifier and your Volvo authorization to read charging data. It sends the battery charge percentage to the configured vehicle entry in your Tibber account. The self-hosted monitor stores credentials and tokens and may log vehicle charging data. The operator is responsible for protecting these files and controlling access to the monitor.

Availability

The software is provided as is under its project license. Readings and sync can be delayed or interrupted, including when Volvo or Tibber services are unavailable. Check the vehicle or charging equipment directly when you need to confirm its status.

Ending use

You can stop the monitor, remove the Volvo authorization through Volvo, and delete the local credentials and tokens. For questions about the software, see the project repository.
